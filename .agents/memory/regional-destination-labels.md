---
name: Regional destination labels
description: How launch guides model islands and regions that are commonly searched like cities.
---

Keep established destination slugs for broad island and regional guides, but explicitly identify the destination type and name the separate traveller bases in user-facing summaries.

**Why:** Renaming established records to one arbitrary city would break links and misrepresent guides that intentionally span several bases. Leaving them described as city neighbourhoods is geographically inaccurate.

**How to apply:** For Bali, Lombok, Chitwan, Langkawi, Penang, Cappadocia, and similar destinations, preserve the broad destination record while clearly labeling its island/region/state scope and treating listed places as bases rather than neighbourhoods.
